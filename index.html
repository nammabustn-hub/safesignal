About SafeSignal

SafeSignal is an independent personal safety project. Our mission is to make safety tools easier to understand and access through a simple bilingual experience.

Contact Us

For questions, feedback or suggestions, please use the project repository contact details. A working contact form will be added before public launch.

Privacy Policy

1. Account information

Firebase Authentication is used for guest and Google sign-in. Guest accounts use an anonymous Firebase user ID.

2. Emergency contacts

Contact names and phone numbers entered by signed-in users are stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore under that user's account. Access is restricted by Firestore security rules. Users should add contacts only with their consent.

3. Location and alerts

This version does not collect live location or send emergency messages. These features are not active.

4. Data deletion

Contact records can be deleted individually from the contacts section. Account deletion and complete data management will be added before public launch.

5. Third-party services

Firebase Authentication, Google sign-in and Cloud Firestore are third-party services.

Terms & Conditions

1. Prototype

SafeSignal is currently a prototype. SOS, messaging, live location and emergency response features are not yet active.

2. Responsible use

Users must not misuse the service, harass others or submit false emergency reports. Add only contacts who have agreed.

3. Emergency limitations

SafeSignal is not an emergency response service and cannot guarantee that help will arrive. In India, call 112 directly in an emergency.
