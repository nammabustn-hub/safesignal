About SafeSignal

SafeSignal is a personal safety preparedness project. Our goal is to make it easier to keep trusted emergency contacts organised. The current website is an early version and does not provide a real emergency dispatch service.

Privacy

Your contact details are stored in Firebase Firestore under your account. Current access rules restrict contact access to the authenticated account owner. Do not add information without the contact's permission. You can delete saved contacts from this page. Review the actual Firebase configuration and data practices before public launch.

Terms of Use

SafeSignal is provided as an early-stage website for preparedness and demonstration. It is not a replacement for emergency services, medical assistance, or official authorities. Do not rely on this demo to send alerts or request rescue. In India, call 112 directly in an emergency.
