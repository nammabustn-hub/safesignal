About SafeSignal

SafeSignal is a personal safety preparedness project. The current website is an early version and does not provide a real emergency dispatch service.

Privacy

Your contact details are stored in Firebase Firestore under your account. Current access rules restrict contact access to the authenticated account owner. Do not add information without consent. You can delete saved contacts from this page.

Terms and Conditions
