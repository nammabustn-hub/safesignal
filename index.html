About SafeSignal

SafeSignal is an independent personal safety project. Our mission is to make safety tools easier to understand and access through a simple bilingual experience.

Contact Us

For questions, feedback or suggestions, contact our team. A working contact email and message delivery system will be added before public launch.

Privacy Policy

1. Information

The current prototype does not activate account registration, emergency contact storage or live location sharing. When these features are introduced, the information collected and its purpose will be clearly explained.

2. Location permissions

Location access will require clear permission. Users should only share location with people they trust.

3. Security and deletion

Before personal information is collected, security controls, data retention limits and deletion processes must be implemented.

4. Cookies and advertising

If advertising or analytics services are added, this policy will be updated to explain relevant cookies, third-party services and privacy choices.

Terms & Conditions

1. Prototype

SafeSignal is currently a prototype. Its SOS, messaging, location and account features are not yet active.

2. Responsible use

Users must not misuse the service, harass others or submit false emergency reports.

3. Emergency limitations

SafeSignal is not an emergency response service and cannot guarantee that help will arrive. In India, call 112 directly in an emergency.
