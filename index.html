About SafeSignal

SafeSignal is an independent personal safety project. Our mission is to make safety tools easier to understand and access through a simple bilingual experience.

Contact Us

For questions, feedback or suggestions, please use the project repository contact details. A working contact form will be added before public launch.

Privacy Policy

1. Account information

Firebase Authentication is used for guest and Google sign-in. Guest accounts use an anonymous Firebase user ID. Google sign-in may provide basic profile information according to your Google account permissions.

2. Location and contacts

The current version does not collect emergency contacts or share live location. These features will require clear permission and an updated privacy explanation before activation.

3. Security and deletion

Do not submit sensitive information through this prototype. Account deletion and data management options will be provided as account features are developed.

4. Third-party services

Firebase Authentication and Google sign-in are third-party services. Their applicable privacy terms also apply.

Terms & Conditions

1. Prototype

SafeSignal is currently a prototype. SOS, messaging, location and emergency contact features are not yet active.

2. Responsible use

Users must not misuse the service, harass others or submit false emergency reports.

3. Emergency limitations

SafeSignal is not an emergency response service and cannot guarantee that help will arrive. In India, call 112 directly in an emergency.
